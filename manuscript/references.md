## Notes

- **Melé et al. 2015 (GTEx transcriptome)**: Principal GTEx Consortium paper on tissue-level transcriptome variation across individuals. DOI: 10.1126/science.aaa0355
- **GTEx Consortium 2013**: Foundational paper establishing the GTEx project scope and methodology. DOI: 10.1038/ng.2653
- **Izgi et al. 2022 (DiCo framework)**: Describes divergence-convergence framework showing tissue convergence during aging in mice. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.68048
- **Chatsirisupachai et al. 2023**: Documents tissue-specific gene downregulation patterns in aging and cancer. DOI: 10.1186/s12864-023-09756-w
- **Tabula Muris Consortium 2018**: Foundational single-cell atlas of adult mouse tissues with 100,000+ cells across 20 organs. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-018-0590-4
- **Schaum et al. 2020 (Tabula Muris Senis)**: Single-cell atlas characterizing aging across mouse tissues from 1 to 30 months. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-020-2496-1
- **Zou et al. 2022**: Single-cell atlas of *Caenorhabditis elegans* aging (C. elegans, not rat). Calico Life Sciences. DOI: 10.1101/2022.06.15.496201
- **Li & Kong 2025**: Multi-omics aging atlas of female rhesus macaque tissues. Primary publication in *Nature Methods* (2025); transcriptomic data available on Figshare (26963386)
- **Lambert et al. 2018**: Comprehensive census of 1,639 human transcription factors with DNA binding specificity. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2018.01.029
- **Eisenberg & Levanon 2013**: List of 3,804 housekeeping genes uniformly expressed across human tissues. DOI: 10.1016/S0168-9525(13)00089-9
- **Benayoun et al. 2019**: Epigenomic and transcriptomic analysis revealing aging-associated inflammatory responses in mouse tissues. DOI: 10.1101/gr.240093.118
